Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026 · Englishالعربية

In short. Nazzim keeps your academic plan on your phone. You can use the whole app without an account. If you create one, your plan is synced so you can use it on another device and get it back if you lose your phone. We do not track you, we do not sell data, and we do not use advertising networks. The planning features run on your device — your study plan is never sent to an AI provider.

1. Who we are

Nazzim ("we", "the app") is a study planning app for university students, operated from the Sultanate of Oman. For any privacy question, contact privacy@nazzim.app.

3. What we never collect

  • Your contacts, calendar, photos, camera, microphone or location
  • An advertising identifier (IDFA / AAID) — so you will never see a tracking permission prompt
  • Any third-party advertising or analytics SDK
  • The text you write. Task titles, subject names and notes are never included in a usage event. Our events carry only counts and fixed labels
  • Information about you from other apps or websites

4. Where your data lives, and for how long

On your device. Your plan is stored on your phone first and works offline. If you never create an account, it stays on your phone and nowhere else.

On our servers. If you sign in, your plan is also stored with Supabase, our database provider, on servers located in Mumbai, India. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS). Each account is isolated at the database level, so no other user can read your data.

Retention. We keep your data until you delete your account. When you delete it, your account and every row belonging to it are removed from our servers — see section 7.

We do not share, sell or rent your data to anyone else, and we use no data brokers or advertising networks.

6. How the planning features work

The Smart Planner, Rescue Mode, exam readiness and progress insights run entirely on your device. When you type a sentence such as "Statistics exam in 6 days", that sentence is processed on your phone and is not sent to us or to any AI provider. These features work with no internet connection.

If we ever change this, we will update this page and tell you clearly in the app before it takes effect.

7. Your choices and controls

  • Use Nazzim without an account. On the first screen, choose "Explore with a sample semester".
  • Delete your account and all its data. In the app: More → Account → Delete account. This removes your account and everything stored with it from our servers, and clears the app on your device. It cannot be undone. You can also request deletion at nazzim.app/delete-account.
  • Erase this device. If you are not signed in, Settings → Delete account clears the local data.
  • Control notifications. Settings → Reminders: each reminder type can be turned off separately, and all of them can be turned off at once.
  • Access and correction. Everything we hold about you is visible inside the app and editable there.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (such as a copy of your data or a restriction on its use). Write to privacy@nazzim.app and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Children

Nazzim is intended for university students and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Connections use TLS encryption. Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider and never stored or seen in readable form. Each account's data is isolated at the database level, enforced by the database itself rather than by app code. No system is perfectly secure, but we design so that one account can never reach another's data.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. For a change that materially affects your privacy, we will notify you in the app before it takes effect.
